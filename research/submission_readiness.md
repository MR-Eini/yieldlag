# Submission readiness

## Completed computational work

See `protocol.md`, scripts, CSVs and the manuscript for exact experiments and
limitations. GitHub release and package checks establish technical integrity;
they do not establish novelty, user adoption or generalization.

## Evidence required from authors or external participants

- Confirm which external/later data the team has never inspected. Germany is
  labelled external, not untouched. The reserved 2019 year is excluded from
  fitting/selection but already existed in the original archives.
- Supply Poland aggregation lineage, solar derivation/units, fixed-area averaging
  period, original GUS table identifiers and derivative redistribution details.
- Obtain an actual independent user's reproduction; the protocol alone does not
  satisfy this item. No external person has been contacted.
- Confirm affiliation, ORCID, contributors/coauthors, funding/conflicts, and
  human scientific review of all AI-assisted code, results and manuscript.
- Sign in to Zenodo to enable the repository and archive a release. Prepared
  `.zenodo.json` metadata is not a DOI or a deposited record.

## Journal route

Primary proposed venue: Environmental Modelling & Software, after author review
and stronger evidence on genuinely new data. Alternative: SoftwareX for a
focused reusable-software paper. Do not submit overlapping manuscripts
simultaneously. GMD requires a rigorous model description and fitness-for-purpose
evaluation. The R Journal requires CRAN/Bioconductor availability and maturity.
CRAN source preparation/checks do not mean CRAN acceptance or submission.

JOSS currently requires more than six months of active public development.
This repository became public on 2 October 2026. Continue legitimate maintenance
and user-driven iteration; elapsed time alone does not establish eligibility.
Do not backdate commits or fabricate community activity. A separate software
paper must declare related publications. Current policy also requires AI-use
disclosure and human review; AI cannot conduct author-editor/reviewer dialogue
except translation.
